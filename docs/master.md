KeMonate Website Master Prompt
Create a modern, mobile-first, responsive web application called KeMonate (Setswana for "cool", "nice", or "wonderful").

Project Vision
KeMonate is an AI-powered platform that helps communities discover, document, preserve, and share Africa's hidden artists, storytellers, musicians, poets, cultural custodians, and oral histories before they are lost.

The platform is inspired by the idea that we know great artists such as JH Pierneef because their work was documented and preserved. Across Africa, there are likely thousands of equally talented creators whose stories, artwork, music, and knowledge may never be captured or shared beyond their local communities.

As AI increasingly influences how content is created, discovered, and consumed, there is a risk that voices not already represented online will remain invisible. KeMonate exists to change that.

The goal is not to use AI to replace creativity. The goal is to use AI to preserve, celebrate, amplify, and share authentic human stories while keeping creators at the center of the process.
